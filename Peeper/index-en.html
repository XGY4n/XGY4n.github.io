PEEPER

PIXEL · 3D · TOP-DOWN · PSYCHOLOGICAL HORROR

CULTS · RITUALS · CONSPIRACY

While the shadow of the Vietnam War still lingers over America, evil stirs quietly in the depths of a dark city.

A rainy night, America at the tail end of the Vietnam War.

You wake suddenly in an alley on a rainy night.

You don't know why you're here, and you cannot fully recall what happened before you woke.

A desire beyond all control is quietly stirring.

A specially crafted 3D pixel look

The entire 3D world of the game is rendered in a carefully designed pixelated style.

02 / Movement

A pixel world in motion

The game uses a fixed orthographic top-down angle, much like classic titles such as old Diablo and old Baldur's Gate.

03 / Interiors

Indoor and outdoor rendering

Indoors and outdoors are rendered with different strategies, which gives interior scenes a stronger retro feel.

04 / Graphics tech

Retro pixels meet modern graphics

While preserving the retro feel of a pixel 3D world, a wealth of modern graphics techniques delivers a striking visual style.

Gameplay & Systems

Exploration — Move through and explore an eerie neighborhood under a top-down camera.

Inspection — Examine a painting or an object up close; the clue may be hidden within.

Dialogue — Line by line, uncover the secrets that have been hidden away.

Puzzles — Solve puzzles to push forward and let the true face of the eerie district surface.

Atmosphere — Set in America at the end of the Vietnam War, explore scenes that blend a vivid period feel with a horror-laden atmosphere.
